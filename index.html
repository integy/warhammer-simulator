Rapid Ingress - Warhammer 40,000 deployment, line of sight and terrain tool

Updated for 11th edition! Rapid Ingress is a free browser tool for tabletop Warhammer 40,000. Place unit bases on a 60 x 44 inch battlefield, test deployment zones, and check line of sight (LOS) in seconds. Measure base-to-base and point-to-point distances as you plan your moves. Supports circle, oval and rectangle bases from 25mm to 170 x 109mm.
